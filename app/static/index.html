Calculation notes

Modern decans use 10° divisions and the successive signs of the same element, with Pluto, Uranus and Neptune as modern rulers. Composites use short-arc ecliptic midpoints. Progressions use one ephemeris day per 365.242190402 elapsed days.

Events are numerical roots of apparent longitude or speed. The displayed second is solver resolution, not a promise of physical accuracy. Client data stays on your computer. No analytics or cloud requests; only the explicit data download uses the internet.

Date & location

West and south are negative. Coordinates and time zone are explicit; verify historical birth records. No location is inferred from a place name.
